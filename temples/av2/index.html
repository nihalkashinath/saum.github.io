Two Sacred Cities. One Seamless Journey.

It takes just 3 days to visit two of the most powerful temples in the world.

Ayodhya

  • Ram Mandir
  • Hanuman Garhi Mandir
  • Dashrath Mahal
  • Kanak Bhavan
  • Saryu Ghat
  • Kalaram Mandir

Varanasi

  • Sri Kashi Vishwanath Mandir
  • Kaal Bhairav Mandir
  • Ganga Aarti (VIP seating)
  • Ganga Boat Ride (Private/Deluxe)
  • Sankatmochan Hanuman Mandir
  • Vishalakshi Mandir (Shakti Peeth)

The 3-Day, 2-Night itinerary also covers other major temples and sacred sites in both cities, sequenced for minimal crowds and unhurried darshans.

If you prefer to cover more activities like food trails, culture walks, museum visits, or special pujas, we can tailor the pace and duration of the trip to your family's needs.

While we suggest covering both these sacred sites in a single, comfortable pilgrimage, we can also help you plan your trip to just one of them, if you so prefer. Similarly, we can organize your travel to include other nearby sacred sites like Gaya, Prayagraj, and others, over more days. You can customise your pilgrimage to the minutest detail!
